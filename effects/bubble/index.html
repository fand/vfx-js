Effects / @vfx-js/effects

BubbleEffect

A foam of soap bubbles filling the silhouette.

Overview

Fills the shape of the element with soap bubbles in thin-film colors. The shape comes from the alpha, so use it on logos or text with a transparent background.

Params

Pass them to the constructor. All of them are optional. Type: BubbleParams

Name Default Description
Bubbles
sourceChannel SourceChannel "alpha" Which channel of the element is read as the silhouette: "alpha" (where it is opaque; default), "luminance" (where it is bright, so the text or the light parts of an opaque image) or "none" (the whole element rect). A change reads the element back again, like refresh().
depth number 0.1 Extrusion depth, relative to the long side of the element (= 2), up to 2. Placement param.
bevel number 0.02 Radius of the rounded edges of the extruded shape, in the same units as depth, up to half of it. The bubbles fill the rounded shape, and a container (containment) rounds them off along its edges. Placement param.
cellSize number 0.1 Size of the big bubbles: their radius is 0.86 of it, in the same units as depth. Smaller bubbles fill the gaps between them (see minSize). Placement param.
cellSizeRandomness number 0.25 How much the big bubbles' radii vary, 0 (all alike) to 1 (each shrunk by a random share, up to all of it). Placement param.
seed number 1 Seed of the random placement. Placement param.
roundness number 0.3 How round the bubbles are, 0 to 1, at the same size: how far apart the big bubbles sit. At 0 they overlap heavily (more of them, cut by flat walls into a polyhedral foam with shallow caps); at 1 their centres are a diameter apart, so they are whole spheres that just touch, with smaller bubbles in the gaps. The filling bubbles cut into their neighbours alike. Placement param.
minSize number 0.25 The smallest bubbles, as a share of cellSize, 0.05 to 1: gaps between the big bubbles, and along the outline, are filled with smaller ones while an empty ball of this size fits. Smaller gives more, smaller bubbles; 1 turns the fill off. Placement param.
containment number 0 How much the shape acts as a container, 0 to 1. 0 lets the bubbles poke out of the silhouette (by 0.3 cellSize at most) and bulge out of its front and back. Above 0, what pokes out is squashed towards the shape, keeping its roundness: a cap that stood h above the shape stands (1 - containment) h. At 1 the bubbles are pressed flat against the shape, like a foam blown up inside a mould. They are also blown up a little, which closes the smallest gaps and leaves creases between them. Values between 0 and 1 cost a little more than either end.
wobble number 0.06 Amplitude of the bubbles' slow drift, relative to each bubble's radius, 0 to 0.15: the walls breathe. Changing it rebuilds the grid the shader walks (cheap).
speed number 0.15 Speed of the drift, in cycles per second.
maxLayers number 6 Films composited per pixel, front to back, 1 to 16. A ray through n bubbles crosses about n + 1.
Film
iridescence number 0.5 Interference colour, 0 (colourless reflection) to 1 (the full thin-film colours).
thickness number 420 Mean thickness of the films, in nm. The colours cycle with it.
thicknessVariation number 0.35 Swirls in the thickness, 0 to 1, as a share of thickness.
drain number 0.5 Drainage, 0 to 1: films are thinner at the top of each bubble and thicker at the bottom, by this share of thickness.
swirlScale number 1.2 Size of the swirls: noise frequency per cell size.
swirlSpeed number 0.12 Speed at which the swirls flow down and change, per second.
reflection number 1.4 Strength of the reflected light, without changing transparency.
rim number 1 The bright white rim of a bubble, 0 to 1. A film seen at a grazing angle reflects much more, in white. 1 is the real film; 0 keeps the interference colours all the way to the edge.
tint string "#6d8ee8" Colour the soapy water gives the light it scatters (a CSS hex colour). Films seen at a grazing angle, and stacks of them, take it on: the depth cue of a foam.
tintStrength number 0.3 How much each film takes on tint, 0 to 1.
opacity number 0 0 to 1: the bubbles' first faces turn into a matte, softly lit body of color. 1 gives opaque balls.
color string "#2f7fe0" Colour of the body that opacity blends in (a CSS hex colour).
Borders
borderWidth number 0.06 Width of the borders on the foam's outside, where two bubbles meet, as a share of cellSize, 0 (off) to 0.5: the water gathered there. The film thickens over this width, by 100 nm per 0.01 at the border, so wider borders show more bands of colour. The white line (see borderHighlight) is a sharp core about half as wide in all. Walls inside the foam get no border.
borderHighlight number 0.35 Brightness of the white line along the borders: the light the water gathered there scatters, 0 (off) to 2.
Backdrop
backdrop BackdropPatternName | BackdropImageSource "none" The backdrop: a pattern name, or an image (an element, an ImageBitmap or a URL). Films reflect it and, with backdropVisible, it is drawn behind the foam; otherwise the page shows through it.
backdropColors BackdropPaletteName | readonly string[] "mono" The palette of a pattern, as GlassParams.backdropColors.
backdropVisible boolean false Draw the backdrop behind and around the foam.
backdropAround boolean false Reflect the backdrop all around, not only behind: the films facing the viewer reflect it too, in place of the even sky. The lights still add their highlights.
backdropScale number 0.25 Size of the pattern's features, or of the image.
backdropDistance number 0.6 Distance of the backdrop plane behind the shape's centre.
backdropSpeed number 0.15 Speed of the pattern, in pattern periods per second.
backdropRatio number 0.5 Share of the second colour of a two-colour palette, 0 to 1.
backdropSoftness number 0.03 Softness of the edges between the palette's colours, 0 to 1.
Lights
lightIntensity number 1.2 Brightness of the soft box lights around the shape, and of a dim sky over it, which the films reflect. 0 turns them off.
lightSoftness number 0.8 Softness of the lights' edges, 0 to 1.
View
yaw number 20 Camera yaw in degrees. 0 looks straight at the front cap; positive turns the front cap to the right.
pitch number 11.5 Camera pitch in degrees. 0 looks straight at the front cap; positive turns the front cap down, showing the top.
orbitAnimation number 11.5 Amplitude of the orbit animation, a slow back-and-forth swing of the camera, in degrees of yaw (the pitch swings 0.3 times as far). 0 keeps the camera still (pointer drags still orbit it when drag is on).
orbitSpeed number 3.3 Speed of the orbitAnimation, as a multiple of the base speed: at 1, a yaw swing takes about 48 seconds. 0 holds the animation where it is. A change while it runs carries on from there.
fov number 30 Field of view in degrees, measured across the element's long side. The camera moves so the z = 0 plane keeps the element's size, so this only changes the strength of the perspective (like a dolly zoom).
zoom number 0.75 Camera zoom. 1 = the z = 0 plane matches the element size.
drag boolean false Orbit the camera by dragging the pointer. A drag starts on the element's box and can then leave it. While on, it listens for pointer events on window, and a drag that orbits keeps the browser from dragging the element (an image, a link) or selecting its text; UI on top of the element that should not orbit can stop pointerdown propagation.
cameraOrigin "element" | "viewport" "element" Where the camera stands:
pointer BubblePointer "none" How the foam reacts to the pointer hovering over the page:
pointerStrength number 1 Strength of the pointer interaction, 0 to 100 (default 1). With the effect's pointer at "tilt", the shape turns to face the pointer by up to 20° × pointerStrength (capped at 90°), reached when the pointer is half a viewport away from the element's centre, and eases back to the rest pose once the pointer leaves the page; 0 turns it off. The tilt adds to yaw / pitch, the orbitAnimation and drags. Other pointer modes say what it does for them.
Output
resolution number 1 Render scale, 0.25 to 2. Below 1 is cheaper and softer.
antialias boolean true Smooth the remaining stair steps with FXAA.
pad number | "fullscreen" 100 Padding around the element, in CSS px, or "fullscreen" to draw on the whole canvas. Default 100. Use "fullscreen" with cameraOrigin: "viewport", where an element off the centre leans out of its rect; it costs full-canvas passes per element.
debug BubbleDebugView "off" Show a debug view instead of the foam: texture lookups per pixel ("cost": black 0, blue 64, green 128, yellow 192, red 256, white 320 or more), films shaded ("layers", 1.6 per colour step), or the bubbles in flat colours ("cells").

API

  • new BubbleEffect( initial?: Partial<BubbleParams> = {}, opts?: BubbleEffectOptions = {}, ): BubbleEffect
  • refresh(): void Capture the element's silhouette again, for when its content changed: an image's src swapped, a text edit.
  • setParams(updates: Partial<BubbleParams>): void
