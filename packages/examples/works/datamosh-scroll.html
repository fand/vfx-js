Morning / Park path

Afternoon / Ramp

Drop in or go home.

The quarter pipe at the old yard is steeper than it looks, and everyone knows it.

The first drop is all in the head. Lean forward and trust the wheels.

We count falls instead of tricks. Eleven before three o'clock.

“Commit. The ramp can tell when you don't.”

Golden hour / Flat ground

Everything looks like a photo now.

Around six the light goes low and orange, and even the easy tricks look expensive.

The cameras come out. The kickflip lands on the fourth go.

Shadows stretch twice as long as we are. Nobody checks the time.

“Golden hour forgives everything except bad footwork.”
