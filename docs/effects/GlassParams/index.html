API Reference / @vfx-js/effects

Type

GlassParams

effects/src/glass.ts:81 ↗ 
import type { GlassParams } from "@vfx-js/effects";

Properties

absorption 

absorption: number

How fast the glass takes its tint with thickness. 0 is clear.

backdrop 

backdrop: GlassPatternName | GlassImageSource

What is seen through the glass: a pattern name, or an image (an element, an ImageBitmap or a URL).

backdropColors 

backdropColors: GlassPaletteName | readonly string[]

The palette of a procedural pattern: a GlassPaletteName or two or more #rrggbb colours in order, each with an equal share of the area. One colour is a flat fill. Stripes, marble bands and the flow go round the colours, the last running into the first; the grid nests them as concentric squares.

backdropDistance 

backdropDistance: number

Distance of the backdrop plane behind the shape's centre.

backdropRatio 

backdropRatio: number

Share of the area that the second colour covers when the palette has two colours, 0 to 1: the width of the stripes and marble bands, of the grid's lines. Ignored for three or more colours.

backdropScale 

backdropScale: number

Size of the pattern's features, or of the image, in the same units as depth.

backdropSoftness 

backdropSoftness: number

Softness of the edges between the palette's colours, 0 to 1. 0 is hard bands; near 1 the pattern is a continuous gradient through the colours.

backdropSpeed 

backdropSpeed: number

Speed of the pattern, in pattern periods per second. The patterns scroll, except "marble" and "flow", which morph in place.

backdropVisible 

backdropVisible: boolean

Draw the backdrop around the glass too, not only through it.

bevel 

bevel: number

Size of the bevel on the edges, in the same units as depth. Most of the dispersion shows on it, where the surface turns.

bevelShape 

bevelShape: GlassBevelShape

Profile of the bevel.

bounces 

bounces: number

Reflections inside the glass before the ray is forced out, 1 to 6.

bulge 

bulge: number

How far the caps rise in the middle, in the same units as depth. 0 keeps them flat; more makes a lens or a gem.

bulgeWidth 

bulgeWidth: number

Distance from the silhouette's edge over which the caps rise to the full bulge. Where the shape is narrower than twice this, the bulge has a crease along its middle.

bump 

bump: number

Ripples on the surface, 0 to 1, like hammered or old glass. Only the normals are bent; the outline stays the same.

bumpScale 

bumpScale: number

Size of the ripples, in the same units as depth.

debug 

debug: GlassDebugView

Show the cost of each pixel instead of the glass, as a heat map of SDF lookups: black 0, blue 64, green 128, yellow 192, red 256, white 320 or more.

depth 

depth: number

Extrusion depth, relative to the long side of the element (= 2).

dispersion 

dispersion: number

Spread of the index of refraction over the spectrum: red is ior - dispersion / 2, violet ior + dispersion / 2. Real glass is around 0.02; 0.1 to 0.2 gives the exaggerated rainbow look.

drag 

drag: boolean

Orbit the camera with pointer drags.

fov 

fov: number

Vertical field of view in degrees.

frost 

frost: number

Roughness of the surface, 0 to 1: what is seen through the glass, and reflected on it, blurs with its distance from the surface.

fxaa 

fxaa: boolean

Smooth the remaining stair steps with FXAA. Refraction on a narrow bevel changes faster than the pixels can follow; this is the cheap fix, resolution: 2 the thorough one.

ior 

ior: number

Index of refraction at the middle of the spectrum.

jitter 

jitter: number

Per-pixel offset of the wavelengths, 0 to 1. Trades the banding of a low samples for fine noise.

lightIntensity 

lightIntensity: number

Brightness of the soft box lights around the shape. 0 turns them off.

lightSoftness 

lightSoftness: number

Softness of the lights' edges, 0 to 1.

orbit 

orbit: number

Amplitude of a slow camera wobble, in radians.

pad 

pad: number | "fullscreen"

Padding around the element, in CSS px, or "fullscreen" to draw on the whole canvas.

paths 

paths: number

Rays traced through the glass per pixel, 1 to 4, spread over the spectrum. The cost grows linearly. 2 is enough for a small dispersion; use more when the rainbow breaks up.

pitch 

pitch: number

Camera pitch in radians. 0 looks straight at the front cap.

reflection 

reflection: number

Strength of the reflection on the outer surface.

relief 

relief: number

Height of a relief on the front cap, in the same units as depth, from the element's luminance: positive raises the bright parts, negative the dark ones (by sinking the bright ones). It makes a button's label or a photo's content show as shape. 0 is off. Read once with the silhouette, so a video gives a still relief.

reliefBlur 

reliefBlur: number

How much the relief's height map is blurred, in the same units as depth. More calms a busy photo and rounds a label's edges. Changing it rebuilds the height map on the CPU.

resolution 

resolution: number

Render scale, 0.25 to 2. Below 1 is cheaper and softer; 2 supersamples the silhouette's edge.

samples 

samples: number

Wavelengths in the spectrum, 1 to 16. They are interpolated between the traced paths, so they are cheap. Equal to paths, the channels split into separate bands instead of a smooth rainbow.

tint 

tint: string

Colour of the glass, #rrggbb. Thicker glass is more saturated.

yaw 

yaw: number

Camera yaw in radians. 0 looks straight at the front cap.

zoom 

zoom: number

Size of the shape relative to the element.
